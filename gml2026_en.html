1. Gemini & the new foundation
The opening message was about pace: "a decade's worth of innovation in the last year alone." Gemini Omni Flash, Google Antigravity, and Gemini Spark are the latest models.
$180–190B in capex this year — Google presented that investment as its structural advantage. There's now an AI agent to help build campaigns directly; execution is increasingly handed off to Gemini.
⌕
2. Search: AI Mode is the new search
Search is no longer just text — it now accepts images, audio, and dialogue. The search box got its biggest redesign in 25 years.
Without AI Max, you don't show up in AI Mode. Ads are generated dynamically to respond to each specific query: "the best ads must be answers."
New formats: Direct Offers (exclusive discounts and coupons surfaced at the moment of purchase intent) and Business Agent for Leads — users can ask a question directly inside the ad and get an answer pulled from the advertiser's site. Currently testing in education, automotive, and real estate.
SEO best practices remain critical: specific content, real reviews, testimonials, and first-hand experiences. Generic content won't rank.
◈
3. AI Brief & Ask Advisor — control back to the marketer Key announcement
AI Brief: describe your brand in plain language — tone, what to avoid, audience priorities — and Gemini uses it to generate creative guidelines for the campaign. Feeds directly into AI Max and PMax. Brief writing becomes a core skill for the team.
Enables A/B testing not just of ad copy, but of entire value propositions — a major strategic unlock.
Ask Advisor: a conversational agentic interface that unifies Google Ads, Analytics, Google Marketing Platform, and Merchant Center. Ask "why did my ROAS drop?" and it pulls insights across all platforms to answer.
✧
4. Asset Studio — creative at scale with AI Key announcement
Asset Studio is the unified creative workspace inside Google Ads. Generate images and video by describing what you need, pulling from existing brand materials and your AI Brief as input.
Powered by Gemini Omni Flash: any input, any output — text to video, image to copy, brief to campaign. Includes 1-Click Creative Testing to test full value proposition variants.
▶
5. YouTube: from reach to real conversion
2B+ people reached through Shorts alone. The message was clear: YouTube now delivers branding AND performance. "Stop choosing between brand building and conversions."
Ask YouTube arrives this summer (US): users can ask questions directly inside the platform. Big opportunity for brands with educational or product content.
Demand Gen expands to Google Maps (great for brick-and-mortar) and adds tablets + YouTube Pause Ads with product feeds.
⊡
6. Agentic commerce — Google as the transaction layer Key announcement
Universal Cart: a cross-merchant cart (Wayfair, Nike, etc.) that tracks price changes and back-in-stock alerts. Checkout on Google or transfer to the merchant's site. Started rolling out in the US on May 19.
Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP): an open standard co-developed with Shopify, Etsy, Wayfair, Target, Walmart, Amazon, Meta, Microsoft, Salesforce, Stripe, Visa, and Mastercard.
For retailers: this is the most structurally disruptive announcement of the event. AI closes the gap between "wanting to buy" and "buying."
◎
7. Measurement — from attribution to prediction Key announcement
Meridian — Google's Marketing Mix Modeling (MMM) tool is now integrated inside Analytics 360. Changes how medium-term measurement works.
The measurement model shifts from backward-looking attribution to predictive signals — "Qualified Future Conversions."
Google Tag Gateway for greater control and quality of first-party data.
